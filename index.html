How to play

  1. Click a tile to place the highlighted pipe.
  2. Click an empty pipe again to swap it (−25 points).
  3. Guide the water from the red valve into the green drain, entering from its open side.
  4. Each pipe the water fills is worth 50. Crosses can be filled twice.
